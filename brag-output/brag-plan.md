# Brag Plan: Rovo

## What is this app?
Rovo lets people launch markets around X profiles, scout other creators, and trade profile tokens on Robinhood Chain.

## The angle
Treat the product's unusually direct line, “Put a person on the market,” as the hook, then show the actual profile launch flow. The edit is confident, spare, and rooted in Rovo's black and lime interface.

## Hook
“Put a person on the market.” arrives in large type against black. The Rovo lime enters as a precise highlight.

## Key moments
- The real Self-Rove / Scout choice.
- A fictional profile is selected, then paired with USDC in a recreation of the launch UI.
- The Identity → Pair → First Buy → Review progression resolves on the Review step.

## Outro
“Make a market around a profile.” Rovo logo and rovo.fun.

## User flow worth showing
Choose Scout → select a fictional public profile and pair asset → review a launch. This is a demonstration of the interface, not a claim that a transaction occurred. Fictional profile details avoid using anyone's real identity.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: confident market launch film
- Interpretation: short cuts, restrained motion, generous reading time, and no invented statistics.

## Format: landscape — 1280x720
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #000000
- Accent: #ccff00
- Text: #ffffff
- Display font: system sans / Arial fallback
- Body font: system sans / Arial fallback
- Strongest visual element: dark rounded launch card with lime Self-Rove / Scout tabs and step indicator.

## Share copy (draft)
Put a person on the market. Rovo lets you launch, scout, and trade profile tokens on Robinhood Chain.

## Audio direction
- Role: low, steady bed with sparse interface accents.
- Music: bundled Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 12.
- Music treatment: low volume with a short fade at the end.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at 109.96 BPM; major moments near 8.74s and 17.47s. Sequential UI reveals follow slower subdivisions to preserve reading time.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle lime edge glow on selected beats.
- SFX posture: sparse clicks and one soft reveal.
- Audio-coupled moments: Scout tab selection, pair selection, Review arrival.
- Restraint rule: the sounds support the UI; they never compete with the copy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The proposition — 3s
Large “Put a person on the market.” with a small Rovo mark and a lime rule.
Sequential/interaction: headline enters, then holds.
Audio intent: immediate but measured.
Audio-coupled idea: soft reveal at headline entry.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Choose the path — 4s
Recreated Rovo launch card. Self-Rove and Scout tabs appear. A cursor selects Scout and the exact line “Scout someone before they launch.” becomes prominent.
Sequential/interaction: Scout selection at about 5s.
Audio intent: quiet click.
Audio-coupled idea: simulated UI interaction.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Give the market an identity — 5s
Fictional creator profile preview appears in Identity. “Pair with” follows, and USDC is selected in the dark card UI.
Sequential/interaction: profile card, then pair card, each holds.
Audio intent: two light interface accents.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card selection.
Transition mood: clean cut → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Review the launch — 5s
Step indicator advances Identity → Pair → First Buy → Review. A review card shows the fictional profile, USDC pair, “First buy: Skipped,” and the lime Launch button. It stops before any transaction.
Sequential/interaction: progress advances, review settles.
Audio intent: understated arrival.
Audio-coupled idea: Review reveal near a strong music cue.
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Rovo — 4s
Rovo logo with “Make a market around a profile.” and rovo.fun. Hold cleanly for sharing.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: music resolves and fades.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft logo accent.
Transition mood: end.

**Music mood for this video:** polished.
**Audio summary:** steady low music, three restrained UI accents, gentle ending.
